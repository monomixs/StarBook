# Privacy Policy

StarBook is built as an Open Source service. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## Information Collection and Use

**StarBook does not collect any personal information.**

Unlike many other apps, StarBook is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy:

- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or crash reports.
- **No Accounts:** You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use the app.
- **No Tracking:** There are no trackers, ads, or analytics integrated into the application.

### Local Data Storage
Any information required for the app to function (such as your playback positions, bookmarks, and sleep timer settings) is stored **locally on your device only**. This data is never uploaded to any server and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

To provide its features, StarBook requires the following permissions:

- **Storage / Media Access:** To scan and play the audiobook files stored on your device.
- **Notifications:** To show playback controls in the notification area and on the lock screen.

These permissions are used solely for the functionality of the app on your device.

## Third-Party Access
StarBook does not share any information with third-party services. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to share.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by opening an issue on the project's repository.
